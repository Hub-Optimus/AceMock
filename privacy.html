AceMock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Operated by Hub Optimus (India)

AceMock is an AI study and interview-prep app. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions? Email SUPPORT_EMAIL.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are stored hashed. We never see your plain password.
  • Content you give the app: text, PDF, DOCX or image files, and CV/resume text you upload to generate quizzes, notes or interview questions, plus answers you type for CV interview feedback.
  • Your results: quiz attempts and scores, notes you generate, and simple usage counts (for example, how many quizzes you generated) that are saved to your account.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos library, or device identifiers. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs in the app.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your quizzes, notes and progress in your account.
  • To generate quizzes, notes, interview questions and feedback from the content you provide.
  • To keep the service working, secure and fair (for example, usage limits).

3. Sharing and AI processing

To generate results, the content you submit is sent from our server to AI providers: OpenAI, Groq and, if you choose it, Anthropic. They process it only to return a response. Text in images is read on your device (OCR) before being sent. Our service runs on Vercel (hosting) and Supabase (database and sign-in). We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Please do not upload sensitive documents (for example, government ID numbers or bank details).

4. Storage and security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with our database provider. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Retention and deleting your data

We keep your account data until you ask us to delete it. To delete your account and all data linked to it (quiz attempts, notes, usage records), email SUPPORT_EMAIL from your registered email with the subject "Delete my AceMock account". We will delete it within 30 days. You can also see the account deletion page.

6. Children

AceMock is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time by emailing us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the app means you accept the update.
