# AceMock — Google Play listing (copy-paste)

**App name:** AceMock: AI Exam & Interview Prep
**Short description (80 max):** Turn any PDF, notes or CV into quizzes, notes and mock interview practice.

**Full description:**
AceMock turns your study material into practice. Upload a PDF, DOCX, photo or paste text, and AI creates quizzes, concise notes and answers in seconds.

Preparing for a job? Upload your CV and get interview questions tailored to your skills, then type your answers and get a score with feedback.

Features:
- Quizzes from any file or image
- AI-generated notes
- CV interview prep with answer feedback
- Progress tracking
